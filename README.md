## Direction Lock

Website creation, Shopify setup, website refresh packages, generic client website builds, third-party website audit/rebuild services, and managed website-care plans are retired as core public Auralis offers unless explicitly reopened by a later strategic decision.

Shopify may remain in use as commerce infrastructure for checkout, payments, product management, merch, creative-product sales, and artist collaboration products. Shopify is infrastructure, not the public service identity.

## Lucid Creations

Lucid Creations is the visual-art / creative-studio identity inside Auralis Digital.

Primary role:

- original sacred geometry,
- psychedelic / visionary art,
- live and music-reactive visuals,
- artist visualizers,
- album / track artwork,
- commissions,
- merch / prints derived from visual work.

It should not be forced to become the final consumer-facing name of the music platform.

## Live Music Platform

The current product direction is:

> Independent artist discovery + explicit non-exclusive permission + coherent DJ-style programming + original music-reactive visuals + continuous live distribution + artist attribution / direct links + future listener interaction.

The livestream is the first distribution surface, not the entire product.

## Shop / Commerce

The React/Vite site and Shopify stack may continue to support creative commerce.

Current commerce principles:

- Shopify remains the checkout/payment/order/product backend where useful.
- Auralis may display product information and route buyers into Shopify checkout.
- Lucid Creations products, RJ Print products where still supported, merch, and artist collaborations may remain commerce surfaces.
- Commerce should support the creative ecosystem rather than define Auralis as a web-development or Shopify-services agency.

Commerce readiness and catalog truth should be verified through the operational docs before major promotion.

## Custom Creative Work

Custom work should stay aligned with the creative ecosystem, especially:

- commissioned artwork,

- artist visualizers,
- visual assets,
- music-related artwork,
- related creative commissions.

Generic website-building services are not part of this active offer.
